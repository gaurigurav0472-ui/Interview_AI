The STAR Method

Use this framework for all behavioral questions to give structured, compelling answers.

S
Situation
Set the scene and give context
T
Task
Describe your responsibility
A
Action
Explain what you did
R
Result
Share the outcome achieved
🎯
Be Specific

Avoid vague answers. Use real numbers, dates, and outcomes. Instead of "I improved sales", say "I increased sales by 30% in Q3 2023."

⏱️
Keep It Concise

Aim for 1–2 minute answers. Practice timing yourself. Rambling loses the interviewer's attention — stay focused and on-point.

🔍
Research the Company

Know the company's mission, products, and recent news. Mention specific things you admire — it shows genuine interest and preparation.

💪
Show Confidence

Speak clearly, maintain eye contact, and avoid filler words like "um" or "uh". Confidence is as important as the content of your answer.

❓
Ask Good Questions

Always prepare 2–3 questions for the interviewer. Ask about team culture, growth opportunities, or what success looks like in the role.

📝
Practice Out Loud

Reading answers is not enough. Practice speaking them aloud — record yourself, review it, and improve. This builds fluency and reduces nerves.
